Help & Support

About us

BMU Campus Eats is a student food-ordering project for BML Munjal University.

Contact

For food availability and collection, contact the restaurant at its campus counter. University support contact details are awaiting confirmation.

Order support

Find your order ID in My Orders and share it with the shop when asking for help.

Forgot your password?

Email-based password reset is not available yet. Contact the Campus Eats project administrator for help accessing your account.

If you remember your password, you can sign in using either your BMU email address or student ID.

Frequently asked questions

How do I register?

Use your BMU email address and student ID on the Sign Up page.

How do I pay?

The current demo supports pay at pickup. Online payment and hostel delivery are proposed features.

Project terms

Orders in this student demonstration are stored for project evaluation. Confirm availability and fulfilment directly with the restaurant.
